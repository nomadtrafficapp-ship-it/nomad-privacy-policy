N.O.M.A.D Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

This policy describes how N.O.M.A.D ("the app") handles your data. The sections below describe the Android app; the companion Windows app, N.O.M.A.D Desktop, is covered in its own section.

What the app collects

While you have a trip running, the app records:

  • GPS location, continuously, including while the app is in the background or the screen is off. This is required to build a timeline of your trip (moving vs. stopped segments, speed, route) and to identify the roads and traffic signals along it.
  • Accelerometer readings, used only to compute acceleration/deceleration for each segment — not stored as raw sensor data.

No data is collected unless you have explicitly started a trip.

How your data is used

Your recorded trip data is used entirely on your own device to:

  • Build a road-level breakdown of each trip (moving/stopped segments, distance, speed, acceleration).
  • Identify the roads and traffic signals your trip passed, and grade any delay against standard traffic-engineering thresholds.
  • Let you export a trip as CSV, XLSX, or GPX to your device's Downloads folder for your own use in other tools (spreadsheets, GIS software, Google Earth), or share those same files directly — for example by email — through your device's own share/send feature. The app only creates or sends these files when you explicitly choose to export or share a trip.
  • An advanced "Export database" option (Settings → Data management) saves a copy of the app's entire local database — every recorded trip, its GPS trace and segments, plus small internal diagnostic/cache tables — as a single database file in your device's Downloads folder, for your own inspection with database tools. Like other exports, this file is created only when you choose to create it, and the app only sends it anywhere when you use "Send to desktop", below.
  • A "Send to desktop" option (Settings → Send to desktop) transfers that same database straight to your own PC running N.O.M.A.D Desktop: over your local network, only after you enter the pairing code shown on the PC; or over a USB cable, either by leaving the file in your device's Downloads folder for the PC to collect once you choose File transfer, or directly if you have USB debugging turned on. It goes only between your own devices, never through us or any server we control, and only when you choose to send it. Network transfers are not encrypted, so use a network you trust, such as your home Wi-Fi.

Third parties your location is shared with

To identify roads and traffic signals, coordinates from your trip are sent to the following public, third-party map services, for the duration of each lookup only:

ServicePurpose
OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine, router.project-osrm.org)Matches your raw GPS trace to the actual road network and identifies road names.
Overpass API (OpenStreetMap query service)Looks up nearby traffic signals, pedestrian crossings, and cross streets.
Nominatim (OpenStreetMap)Reverse-geocodes a coordinate to a place/road name as a fallback.
OpenStreetMap tile server (tile.openstreetmap.org)Loads the map imagery shown on the trip playback screen — receives only the map tile coordinates for the area being viewed, not your GPS trace itself.

These are public OpenStreetMap-ecosystem services, not operated by us. We do not operate any backend server of our own, and your trip data is never uploaded to one — only the specific coordinates needed for a lookup are sent to these services, and only when a lookup is actually needed.

If you select a regional traffic-signal dataset in Settings, signal lookups for that region are resolved entirely offline from data bundled with the app, with no network request at all.

Data storage and retention

All trip data (GPS traces, segments, exports) is stored locally on your device only, in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any server we control. You can delete individual trips at any time from Trip History; deleting the app removes all of its data.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kits (SDKs). Nothing about your usage of the app is sent to us.

N.O.M.A.D Desktop (Windows)

N.O.M.A.D Desktop is a companion app for Windows PCs for reviewing and comparing trips recorded with the Android app. It handles your data as follows.

  • No recording. The desktop app never collects your location. It only shows trips you choose to transfer to it from the phone.
  • Transfers stay between your devices. Trips go straight from the phone to your PC, over your local network or a USB cable. Nothing passes through us or any server we control. The PC only accepts trips while its Receive page is open: over the network, only from a phone that has entered the pairing code shown on the PC's screen; over a USB cable, only from a phone you have connected and unlocked. You can also import a database file you exported from the phone yourself. A received database replaces the trips loaded on the PC only after you confirm it.
  • The same lookup services as the phone. When the desktop app identifies or re-checks a trip's roads and signals, it sends coordinates from that trip to the same third-party services listed above (OSRM, Overpass API and Nominatim), and loads map imagery from the OpenStreetMap tile server, in the same way as the Android app.
  • Update checks. To find new versions, the app asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release: when it starts, at most twice a day, or when you choose Check for updates. You can turn automatic checks off in About. GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version number. No trip data is sent. Updates are downloaded from GitHub and checked against their published checksum before they are installed.
  • Traffic-signal datasets. If you add a regional traffic-signal dataset, it is downloaded from that region's public open-data portal. No trip data is sent.
  • Where your data is kept. Trips are stored only on your PC, in %APPDATA%\com.nomad\N.O.M.A.D Desktop, together with backup copies of the trips each import replaced. Exports are saved to your Downloads folder, or shared through Windows' own share feature, only when you choose to. You can delete trips in the app. Uninstalling the app leaves your trips in that folder; delete the folder to remove them.
  • No analytics. As with the Android app, the desktop app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and nothing about your use of it is sent to us.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
